# ORM — Review Generation Prompts

Use these prompts to generate review-request messages and review responses for T&M Hauling Bakersfield.

---

## 1. Review-Request Text Message (SMS)

> Write a short SMS for T&M Hauling Bakersfield that thanks a customer for their service and asks for a Google review. The tone should be friendly and direct. Include a link to the Google review page. Keep it under 300 characters.

**Result:**
> Hey [Name], this is [Crew Member] from T&M Hauling. Thanks for letting us clear out your space today. If you're happy with the work, we'd love a quick Google review: https://share.google/05y5uX2DmmRPEeol2. It helps us a ton. — T&M

---

## 2. Review-Request Email

> Draft a short email from T&M Hauling Bakersfield requesting a Google review after a completed job. Include: thank the customer, mention we care about quality, provide the review link. Keep it warm and professional, 3-4 sentences.

**Result:**
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks again for choosing T&M Hauling. We hope you're happy with your clean space — we sure enjoyed working on it.
>
> If you have a moment, a Google review helps other Bakersfield folks find us and tells us we're doing things right.
>
> Leave a review here: https://share.google/05y5uX2DmmRPEeol2
>
> — T&M Hauling

---

## 3. 5-Star Review Thank-You Response

> Write a public reply from T&M Hauling Bakersfield to a 5-star Google review that says: "Fast, professional, and fair price. Would use again." Keep it warm, under 2 sentences.

**Result:**
> Thanks so much — we really appreciate you taking the time to leave this. Glad we could help, and we're here whenever you need us.

---

## 4. 4-Star Review Response

> Write a public reply to a 4-star Google review that says: "Good service, a little slow on arrival but got the job done." Thank them and acknowledge the feedback without being defensive.

**Result:**
> Thank you for the honest feedback — we're glad we got the job done for you. We'll keep working on timing, and we appreciate you giving us the chance.

---

## 5. 3-Star or Below Review Response

> Write a professional, empathetic public response to a 3-star Google review that says: "Left a few small items behind. Called and they came back next day but still." Acknowledge the miss, apologize, and keep it constructive. Do not argue.

**Result:**
> We appreciate you sharing this and giving us the chance to make it right. We've addressed this with the crew to make sure we're more thorough on every job. Please reach out if there's anything else we can do.

---

## 6. Photo-Based Review Request (follow-up)

> Write a follow-up SMS to a customer who already left a review, asking if they'd be willing to add a photo of the completed clean-up to their review (if they have one). Helpful for social proof.

**Result:**
> Hi [Name] — thanks again for the review! If you have a "before and after" photo of the clean-up, adding it to the review would be amazing. Really helps people see what we can do.

---

## Usage

- Copy the prompt header (the `> ...` block) into any AI chat to generate the message.
- Customize the result with the customer's name and job details.
- For review responses, paste the customer's actual review text in place of the example quote.
