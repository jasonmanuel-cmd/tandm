Terms of Service

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Services Provided

T&M Hauling provides professional junk removal, debris hauling, and property clearing services in Bakersfield, CA. All quotes provided online or via phone are estimates. Final pricing is determined on-site by our team.

2. Estimates & Pricing

Every job includes up to three hours of on-site crew labor for the agreed work, regardless of load size. Labor time starts when the crew begins work on site and ends when loading and cleanup are complete.

If the work will take more than three hours, including because the job is larger than described or because of conditions we could not see when estimating, we will stop, explain the additional work, and quote a flat additional-labor fee. We continue only after you approve that fee in writing (signature, text, or email). Work beyond what the approved fee covers requires a new written approval.

The crew confirms the price on site before loading. Price ranges published on this website are estimates only.

Pricing is based on volume (truck space), weight (disposal fees), and labor. T&M Hauling reserves the right to adjust estimates if the actual load differs from the description or photos provided.

3. Photos & Video

T&M Hauling may take photos and video of the work area before, during, and after a job to document site conditions, the items removed, and the completed work. By booking a job, you acknowledge this notice.

We do not record audio of conversations with you, or film you or members of your household, without your consent. Our crew avoids filming personal documents, mail, screens, and other private items.

We only use photos or video of your property in our marketing, such as our website and social media, with your permission. When we do, we will not show faces, house numbers, street names, license plates, or personal items without your separate permission. You can withdraw marketing permission at any time by contacting us, and we will take down posted content of your property.

4. Hazardous Materials

We do not haul hazardous materials, including but not limited to: wet paint, chemicals, biohazards, ammunition, or large quantities of tires without prior arrangement. Customers are responsible for disclosing any hazardous items.

T&M Hauling reserves the right to refuse the hauling of hazardous materials (e.g., chemicals, asbestos, or biological waste) in accordance with Kern County disposal regulations.

5. Scheduling & Cancellations

We strive for same-day service. Cancellations should be made at least 2 hours prior to the scheduled window.

6. Liability

T&M Hauling is not responsible for damage to property during the extraction of items if the extraction requires moving items through narrow or hazardous paths disclosed by the customer.
